## What matters most

- `NULL` is not equal to anything, including itself
- `ORDER BY` is not deterministic without a stable tie-breaker
- window functions evaluate after partitioning and ordering
- `CASE` and `COALESCE` are your best tools for data cleanup

## Safety checklist

Before shipping a query, verify:

1. which columns are nullable
2. how ties are ordered
3. whether the logic must be per partition
4. whether the result depends on engine-specific behavior
