---
title: RANK
description: Assign ranks while keeping tied values in the same rank group — and skipping ahead by the tie count.
---

## Syntax

```sql
RANK() OVER (
  [PARTITION BY partition_expression, ...]
  ORDER BY sort_expression [ASC | DESC], ...
)
```

<Parameters>
  <Param name="PARTITION BY" type="expression, …">
    Optional. Restarts ranking per group. Without it, the whole result set is one partition.
  </Param>
  <Param name="ORDER BY" type="expression [ASC|DESC], …" required>
    Defines rank order. Rows equal under this ordering tie and share a rank.
  </Param>
</Parameters>

## What it does

`RANK()` gives the same rank to tied rows, then **skips ahead** by the number of
ties — the next distinct value's rank equals its row position, not the previous
rank + 1.

Example: values `10, 10, 20` become ranks `1, 1, 3`.

## Compare the three ranking functions

| salary | ROW_NUMBER | RANK | DENSE_RANK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 90,000 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 90,000 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 80,000 | 3 | 3 ← gap | 2 ← no gap |
| 70,000 | 4 | 4 | 3 |

`RANK` is the one to reach for when "how many rows beat this one" matters —
e.g. a leaderboard where two people tied for 1st correctly pushes the next
person to 3rd, not 2nd.

## Example

<SqlRunner query={`SELECT
  name,
  department_id,
  salary,
  RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY department_id ORDER BY salary DESC) AS dept_salary_rank
FROM employees
ORDER BY department_id, dept_salary_rank;`} />

Ada and Grace both earn 145,000 in department 10 — both rank `1`. Linus is next
at rank `3`, not `2`: two rows occupied rank 1, so rank 2 is skipped.

## Filtering by rank

Like every window function, `RANK()` can't go in `WHERE` (it hasn't been
computed at that stage) — wrap it in a subquery or CTE and filter the outer
query. See [DENSE_RANK: filtering by rank](/docs/functions/dense_rank#filtering-by-rank)
for the full pattern; the fix is identical.

## Safety questions

- do ties need to be grouped or differentiated?
- is the ordering stable enough to support repeatable ranking?
- do you need dense ranking rather than standard ranking?
